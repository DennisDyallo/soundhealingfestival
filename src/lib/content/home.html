Step into an immersive Evening where sound becomes sanctuary.

Enter the Church at Skeppsholmen and let yourself be immersed in experience of Sound, Music and Silence as Healing.

You will experience a journey through sounds inspired by nature and the elements: Metal, Wood, Air, Crystal, Skin and Human Voice. To honor the spirit of Scandinavian land we bring Yoik as indigenous expression of Sami people.

​

Both chair and sitting pillow ticket available.
